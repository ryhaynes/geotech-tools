Wn = Σ(σ′avg × Δεv) p′c = OC ∩ NC lines on W vs log σ′c Cεr = Δεv / Δlog σ′c  [OC] Cεc = Δεv / Δlog σ′c  [NC] Cr = (1+e0) · Cεr Cc = (1+e0) · Cεc

ParameterDefinitionNotes
WnCumulative volumetric strain energy = Σ(σ′avg × Δεv), computed via the trapezoidal rule over successive consolidation increments.Units same as σ′c (psf, ksf, or tsf)
p′cPreconsolidation pressure. Identified as the intersection of OC and NC linear regression lines on W vs log σ′c. More objective than Casagrande’s maximum-curvature method.Intersection of two regression lines
Cεr / CεcStrain-based recompression and compression indices. Slopes of εv vs log σ′c on OC and NC segments respectively. No e0 required.Directly from triaxial data; use in strain-based settlement calcs
Cr / CcVoid-ratio-based recompression and compression indices. Cr = (1+e0) × Cεr and Cc = (1+e0) × Cεc. Consistent with conventional e–log p′ framework.Requires e0 input
OC/NC splitUser-defined shared data point that belongs to both regression segments. Minimum 2 points required on each side. Adjust to minimize residuals on the W vs log σ′c plot.Row is shared (overlap point)
