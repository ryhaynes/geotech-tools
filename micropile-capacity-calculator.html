Structural Resistance
Cased & uncased section capacity  ·  Eq. 10.9.3.10.2 & 10.9.3.10.3
§10.9.3.10
▾
Simplified pass — no plunge length credit. Per §10.9.3.10.2/.10.3, the uncased section's demand may be reduced by load transferred to the ground within the plunge length (§10.9.3.10.4). That reduction isn't applied here, so both the cased and uncased sections are checked against the full factored demand — conservative, but simple. Final design should incorporate plunge length once established.
