§3.4.1, §10.6.3.3
Factored Load Inputs (from Structural Analysis, Strength I)
Enter the Strength I factored load resultants provided by the structural engineer, resolved to the base of the footing. These are inputs — the tool uses them to compute eccentricity, effective dimensions B′×L′, and factored contact pressure qu.
Eccentricity is evaluated at the Strength I limit state using factored loads per §10.6.3.3. Limit: e ≤ B/3 (or L/3) for footings on soil.

§10.5.5.2.2, §10.6.3.1.2a
Depth Correction Factor dq
Apply dq only when soils above the bearing elevation are as competent as foundation soils; otherwise dq = 1.0. dq is capped at 1.4 and has been verified for φ = 32–42° and Df/B = 1–8 (AASHTO commentary, §10.6.3.1.2a).
Load Inclination Factors ic, iq, iγ
Per §10.6.3.1.2a Commentary: inclination factors use unfactored H and V. These factors may be overly conservative for Df/B ≥ 1 (Meyerhof, 1953; Brinch-Hansen, 1970). For footings with modest embedment, consideration may be given to omitting inclination factors.
When inclination factors are not applied, ic = iq = iγ = 1.0.
