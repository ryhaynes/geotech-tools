Earth Pressure & Surcharge

Eq. 3.11.5.3-1
Coulomb Active Earth Pressure Parameters
For a vertical stem back face (θ = 90°), Eq. 3.11.5.3-1 reduces to the standard Coulomb formula with Ka = cos²(φb) / [cos(δ) × (1 + √(sin(φb+δ)sin(φb−β) / (cos(δ)cos(β))))²]. For δ = 0 and β = 0, this reduces to the Rankine Ka.
Uniform Surcharge (Optional)
Per §C11.5.6: surcharge load is included over the heel for bearing resistance check only. For eccentricity and sliding, surcharge is applied on the driving side only (additional horizontal thrust), not over the heel as a stabilizing vertical force.
